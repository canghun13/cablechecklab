Boundary reference

USB PD facts used in the charging tools.

These are screening boundaries, not a substitute for the exact source, sink, cable, dock, or compliance record.

Electrical boundaries

BoundaryTool interpretation
Up to 3 AA 60 W cable class can cover a compliant point at 20 V or below.
More than 3 A, up to 5 AA 5 A electronically marked C-to-C cable is required.
More than 20 VThe path requires USB PD Extended Power Range support.
Up to 240 WOverall USB PD ceiling: 48 V × 5 A.

Current USB-IF certified C-to-C power logos use 60 W or 240 W. Legacy 100 W / 5 A cable claims may remain valid for older products, but the new-cable selection tools recommend the current 240 W marking above 60 W.

PPS interpretation

The PPS checker treats one APDO as a rectangular voltage/current boundary plus any explicitly published power ceiling. It tests one requested point against one range. It does not assume that every certified charger includes PPS, and it does not infer the fallback rate when a match is absent.

Dock and hub budgeting

The hub calculator follows Microsoft’s public docking example structure: total supply must cover host delivery and each downstream allocation. A user-entered dock reserve keeps undocumented allocation visible instead of pretending it is a universal constant.

Tool policy

A matching calculation is labeled declared fit or range match. Only hardware or trusted system telemetry can observe the negotiated profile. No tool predicts battery taper, thermal limits, or a proprietary fallback wattage.
