VR Tracking: Sources & Worksheet Boundaries

Reviewed November 5, 2026. Product versions, firmware and applications can differ.

What the finite rules cover

Optical generation, receiver family and station synchronization are separate declared properties. Ultimate is not another classic optical tracker. Multi-channel dongles do not make USB sockets equivalent to radio resources. Runtime and app observations are evidence records, not manufacturer diagnostics.

Scope of each result

  • Optical Tracking Generation Checker: Check one declared device’s optical reference requirement, not the complete headset or accessory system. Start with exact manuals rather than connector shape or a generic SteamVR label.
  • Tracking Receiver Assignment Planner: Allocate free documented radio channels, not USB sockets. This worksheet supports one homogeneous classic-compatible pool and, separately, at most one documented Ultimate dongle with up to five supported trackers.
  • Base-Station Sync Planner: Use this for an already identified station generation. It produces logical synchronization requirements, not a room layout or coverage estimate. Only the first N channel fields apply to N active 2.0 stations.
  • Tracking Evidence Isolator: For a wireless receiver path, record what has actually been observed using the exact device and app manuals. A paired LED, a listed runtime device, a valid tracked pose and an assigned app role are not interchangeable. A direct wired-USB device is outside this pairing-stage model.

Unknown is not an established fit

Where the exact function, runtime, component or capacity is not documented, the result remains conditional. An assignment can be internally consistent without proving physical operation. Copy and print retain the user-entered evidence and the confidence boundary; analytics do not receive those entered values or labels.

Safety and maintenance

No laser inspection, hardware opening, station mounting/coverage certification, firmware change, driver installation, calibration measurement or security bypass is supplied. Follow the exact manufacturer’s current consumer setup and safety instructions. The four-station worksheet limit is not a claim that every device supports four stations.
