USB4 rates, tunnels, and confidence boundaries.

Use these figures to form requirements, not to claim undisclosed product behavior or application throughput.

Windows bandwidth planning

Microsoft’s USB4 design guidance documents explicit allocation behavior, including a maximum reservation of 90% of the link in its connection-manager planning model (for example 18 Gbps on a 20 Gbps link and 36 Gbps on 40 Gbps). DisplayPort bandwidth can be adjusted dynamically, and USB plus PCIe use a shared pool. Cable Check Lab exposes the planning percentage so it is never mistaken for a universal throughput constant.
