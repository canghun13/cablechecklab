A useful order, not four copies of one check

  1. Establish channel and input/output roles before buying a signal cable.
  2. For a headset, choose the split/combine function and receiving TRRS order.
  3. For a microphone, verify the power system separately with equipment off.
  4. If a documented path is noisy, record controlled A/B observations rather than assume a fault.

Headset wiring and microphone power are separate branches; you do not have to run every tool. None checks live voltage, records audio, certifies a device, or predicts sound quality.

Safety boundary

Never join powered outputs, connect speaker power to mic/line inputs, or defeat protective earth. Stop for electrical fault symptoms and use qualified service.
