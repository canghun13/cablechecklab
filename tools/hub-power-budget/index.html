    Use explicit budgets, not device stereotypes

    Microsoft’s docking guidance demonstrates the correct structure: the supply must cover host delivery plus each downstream allocation and dock needs. Actual products may reserve power, cap groups of ports, or reduce host charging when peripherals draw more.

    Copy the manufacturer’s simultaneous limits. If the listing only says “130 W adapter” and “up to 100 W PD,” it has not yet established how much remains for every downstream port.
