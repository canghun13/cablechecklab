    Why there is no exact-model verdict

    Dock compatibility depends on the host port, GPU display count, operating system, dock chipset and firmware, host cable, display modes, power reserve, and active port combination. Public product pages often omit at least one of those facts.

    This builder creates rejection criteria: if a listing never states a required function, do not promote it to “compatible” based on connector shape.
