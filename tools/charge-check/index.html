    How to use the result

    The smallest declared power capability is the electrical ceiling. It is not a prediction of live charging power or of the fallback rate when a protocol is missing. USB Power Delivery negotiation selects a mutually supported profile, while the device controls what it draws.

    Before you enter a wattage

    • Use the output for the exact charger port and active port combination, not only the large total printed on the charger.
    • For more than 60 W, confirm a 5 A electronically marked cable. Current certified cable branding uses 60 W or 240 W markings.
    • For phones, distinguish standard PD, PPS, and proprietary fast charging. A high cable rating cannot add a missing protocol.
    Safety: wattage compatibility is not a product safety certification. Stop using visibly damaged, wet, unusually hot, or counterfeit equipment.
