    A TOSLINK cable is not a format converter

    The optical connector can be used for ADAT or S/PDIF. A preamp sending ADAT into an input selected for S/PDIF is not a stereo connection just because both ports take the same plug. Match the declared digital format and OUT-to-IN direction before changing the cable.

    Example: ADAT OUT → optical S/PDIF IN with a direct cable flags a format conflict. If the destination manual documents a selectable ADAT input, select that mode in the equipment and enter ADAT here. Otherwise a genuine supported format-conversion device is a different purchase from a passive adapter.

    What a matching result leaves open

    Matching formats do not establish the number of exposed channels, high-rate S/MUX support, clock lock, signal routing or S/PDIF codec decoding. This checker is for a digital studio path, not a TV-surround/DAC selector. A passive coupler may add optical loss, and a converter may impose its own sample-rate, mapping or clock constraints. Read exact manuals; no universal cable-length or converter compatibility claim is made.

    Primary context: Focusrite digital I/O formats. Continue with channel capacity or timing only when that is the missing layer.
