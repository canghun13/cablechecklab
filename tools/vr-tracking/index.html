Different decisions, not one repeated verdict

Optical-generation support, radio receiver resources, station synchronization and observed runtime/app stages have different inputs and different next actions. Buying another USB dongle cannot convert a 1.0-only optical sensor, and a paired LED cannot assign an app body role.

Try an error you can actually resolve

Example: three requested classic-compatible devices and three verified total slots fit when none are reserved. Reserve one of those same three slots for an existing controller and one requested device is short. Five spare Ultimate channels cannot fill that classic-pool shortfall.

Offline planning, explicit limits

No connected-device scan, room/laser coverage estimate, automatic tracking diagnosis or coordinate calibration. SteamVR, VIVE and Tundra names identify the documented systems; this independent site is not affiliated with their manufacturers.
