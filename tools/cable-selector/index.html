    Why this is separate from decoding a listing

    The Cable Capability Decoder starts with a product claim and asks what it plausibly means. This selector starts with your workload and creates the minimum claims a candidate cable must meet.

    Power, data, video, and tunneling are independent axes. A 240 W cable may be USB 2.0 only; a fast data cable may not carry 5 A; and connector shape alone proves none of them.
