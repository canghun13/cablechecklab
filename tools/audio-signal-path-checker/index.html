    Why vocals can disappear

    A stereo headphone output carries left and right. A balanced mono receiver expects two representations of one channel and responds to their difference. A straight TRS cable can therefore reduce common center content rather than deliver normal stereo. The amount depends on the program; this tool does not predict a fixed loss in dB.

    Use two inputs for stereo

    Declare a stereo L/R breakout and two independent inputs to plan stereo capture. If you need one mono input, find a documented summing stage. Do not join two outputs with a generic Y cable. Balanced-to-unbalanced wiring is output-topology dependent, so there is no universal contact-short recipe here.

    Example: stereo headphone out → one balanced mono interface input → straight TRS flags a role mismatch. Selecting a suitable L/R breakout and two input channels changes the role result; the exact levels and wiring still need verification.

    For ADAT or optical S/PDIF rather than an analogue signal, use the digital studio workbench to check format, channels, timing and digital routing.
