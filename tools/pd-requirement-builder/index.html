    The important boundaries

    Power equals voltage × current. Standard USB-C cable current tops out at 3 A without a 5 A electronically marked cable. USB PD Standard Power Range stays at 20 V or below; higher EPR profiles extend to 28 V, 36 V, and 48 V, with an overall ceiling of 240 W.

    A result describes the minimum electrical class only. The device, charger, cable, and intermediate dock still need a mutually supported profile.
