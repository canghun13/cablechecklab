Choose the action that is actually blocking you

  • Third-party pad: record the required sensor mode, zones and optional features.
  • More pads than obvious inputs: allocate one equivalent pool and preserve documented split groups.
  • Hi-hat strikes but no opening positions: map the separate controller information path.
  • Missing response: record the earliest observed stage and an approved single-variable test.

These tools do not share one compatibility verdict. Capacity arithmetic cannot prove a pad’s wiring; hearing a strike cannot prove foot-position control; a symptom cannot prove hardware damage.

Start with a bounded example

Two dual-zone pads and four single-zone pads can occupy four equivalent groups only when two remaining groups explicitly support independent single-pad splits. A hi-hat controller is not another such strike group. A digital pad may reserve a corresponding analog assignment. Record those restrictions before counting.

Manual evidence, not a model database

Use the precise pad, module input, firmware, mode and approved lead. No browser hardware sensing, universal Y-lead recipe, resistor modification, calibration prescription or guarantee of playing feel is supplied.
