    What the ratio can and cannot tell you

    The minimum arithmetic ratio is estimated uncompressed payload ÷ usable link payload. VESA describes DSC up to about 3:1 as its common visually lossless compression range, but a ratio below that boundary is not a compatibility promise.

    The source and sink must support DSC, while docks and adapters may need to pass, decode, or re-encode the stream. Exact timings, slices, FEC, and exposed modes remain hardware-specific.
