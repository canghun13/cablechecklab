    Work through a concrete example

    Example: for two documented 1.0 stations, declared unobstructed optical synchronization yields b/c; a declared approved sync-cable route yields A/b. For two 2.0 stations, entering channel 3 twice identifies a collision. Changing the second to an unused channel creates a logical channel plan, not a coverage guarantee.

    Documented 1.0 consumer case

    A single 1.0 station uses mode A. Two use b/c with unobstructed optical synchronization or A/b with an approved synchronization cable, as described in the linked HTC instructions. No three- or four-station 1.0 plan is supplied.

    2.0 channels are not a sync cable

    This worksheet supports up to four active 2.0 stations per consumer tracked set and requires distinct channel IDs from 1–16. The 1.0 route/cable/visibility fields are ignored for a 2.0 result. Actual tracked-device support remains a separate prerequisite.

    No placement or safety certification

    No room coverage, beam geometry, mounting, range or tracking-accuracy result is inferred. Do not open stations, inspect laser emissions or use their firmware port as a normal tracking-data route. Follow exact current manufacturer instructions.
