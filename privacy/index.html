Privacy

Your tool inputs stay in your browser.

Cable Check Lab does not require accounts and does not send calculator form values to a Cable Check Lab server.

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-8PFRRXPGEF) to understand aggregate page usage, such as visited pages, approximate device/browser information, referral source, and interactions provided by Google Analytics. Google may process identifiers and location estimates under its own terms and privacy controls.

Tool inputs

Tool calculations run locally in your browser. Cable Check Lab does not submit the values you enter in tool forms to a Cable Check Lab database. Printing and clipboard actions use browser features you request.

Email

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer the request, investigate a correction, or maintain the site. Do not send sensitive personal or payment information.

Hosting and logs

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the provider may process technical request logs for security, reliability, and delivery.

External links

Reference and product links lead to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. Cable Check Lab is not responsible for those sites.

Your choices

You can use browser privacy controls, content blockers, or Google Analytics opt-out options. Blocking analytics does not prevent the tools from calculating.
